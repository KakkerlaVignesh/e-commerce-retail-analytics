## 2. Bullet Points (For Experience / Projects Section)

- **Engineered an End-to-End Retail Data Pipeline**: Processed and cleaned 112,000+ transaction records from the authentic Kaggle Olist dataset using Python (`pandas`, `sqlite3`) and ANSI SQL, fixing missing values, deduplicating order items, and standardizing ISO timestamps.
- **Advanced SQL Querying & Business Intelligence**: Wrote 7 production-grade SQL queries employing CTEs, Window Functions (`LAG()`, `DENSE_RANK()`, `SUM() OVER()`), and date math (`JULIANDAY`) to analyze revenue trends, payment installment behavior, and category performance across 96,478 delivered orders.
- **Uncovered Strategic Revenue & Logistics Insights**: Identified that top categories (Health & Beauty, Watches) generated R$ 2.46M, while regional logistics analysis revealed a 37.4% revenue concentration in São Paulo (R$ 5.77M, AOV R$ 142.51) and severe delivery delay spikes in northeastern states (AL 23.9%, MA 19.7%).
- **Quantified Delivery Latency Impact on Satisfaction**: Discovered that fulfillment delays caused customer satisfaction review scores to plummet from 4.29 to 2.57 stars while surging 1-star reviews from 6.6% to 46.2%.
- **Designed Interactive Power BI Dashboards**: Built a 2-page executive Power BI dashboard with custom DAX measures (`MoM Revenue Growth %`, `On-Time Delivery Rate %`, `Repeat Purchase Rate %`), interactive slicers, and modern dark-theme visuals.
